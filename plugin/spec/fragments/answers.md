## The user's answers

- **By reference:** the user names an item by its list and number, `{{lists.decide.header}}.2`, an option by its letter, `{{lists.decide.header}}.2: B` or `2. B`, and either by its name. A note may follow the letter, `{{lists.decide.header}}.2.B: <note>`, and words after the colon with no letter, `{{lists.decide.header}}.2: <words>`, answer the item in the user's own words; read either as part of the answer, and a note holding a question as a question about the item. An item of an earlier turn carries that turn's id, from its usage line, before or after: `{{example.turn}}.{{lists.decide.header}}.2` or `{{lists.decide.header}}.2 {{reference.turn-after.0}} {{example.turn}}`; with no id, the last final response is meant. An answer to an item of a turn that {{command.reassess.name}} replaced is read against the item that took it in.
- **Only the user's own text answers:** the prompts they type or send with the {{plugin}} pane's Submit button, and the messages they send while a turn runs; an agent's report, a task notification, a skill's body or another mod's prompt answers nothing, even when it names an item.
- **A question is never a choice.** An answer holding a question mark or any question, `{{lists.decide.header}}.1?`, `B?`, "how would B work?", "why not C?", asks about the item and picks nothing, even when it names an option. Restate the item in its own list, whatever the list, with the answer as the note under its summary and its options rewritten where the answer changes them; the item stays open until the user answers it without a question. A question goes in {{list.answers}} only when it names no listed item.
- **A {{lists.review.header}} item stays open until the user's reply shows they reviewed it:** they comment on it, answer from it, or say they looked; a reply that only answers something else leaves it open.
- **An {{lists.authorizations.header}} item stays open until it is granted or withdrawn:** the prompt is answered or the approval sent, or the session no longer needs it and says so in its {{lists.done.header}} list. A grant the session cannot see, a password typed in the user's own terminal, closes when the user says it is granted, naming it as `{{lists.authorizations.header}}.3`. A prompt that timed out unanswered is raised again and restated in its list, or withdrawn.
